# K0 T3 — kernel models on the new contracts

_Date: 2026-10-21_ · Parent: [`2026-10-21-k0-kernel-spec.md`](2026-10-21-k0-kernel-spec.md)
(§2 model table, §5 row T3, §6 G1/G5/G6/G7).

T3 implements the K0 neuron models and devices under `brainpy_state/_kernel/models/`
on the T1/T2 contracts (`NeuronModel`, `Device`, `SpikeRule`, `Ports`, integrators,
propagators). Nothing is exported publicly.

## 1. Models

| class | integration | inputs (port: kind, unit) | output | states | default integrator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LIF` | exact (`step` override, exponential Euler = exact for the linear ODE) | `I`: Current (mA), `delta`: Event (mV) | `spike`: Event(1) | `V`, `latch_I`, `spike` | `Exact()` |
| `iaf_psc_alpha` | exact propagators (`step` override, T2 JAX propagators) | `ex`: Event (pA), `in_`: Event (pA), `I`: Current (pA) | `spike`: Event(1) | `V_m`, `I_syn_ex`, `dI_syn_ex`, `I_syn_in`, `dI_syn_in`, `latch_I`, `spike`, `refractory_count` | `Exact()` |

Parameter names and defaults follow NEST (NEST models) and `_brainpy/lif.py` (`LIF`).

## 2. Timing (architecture §7.1)

* `event` inputs are jumps applied **after** the step's propagation (`dI_syn += e/tau * w`
  for `iaf_psc_alpha`; `g += w` / `dg += e/tau * w` for the conductance models, whose
  ports are `filtered=True` conductance ports: the neuron owns the kinetics). For `LIF`,
  `delta` jumps are added to `V` **before** the threshold check (BrainPy and NEST
  `iaf_psc_delta` semantics), which is why `LIF` overrides `step`.
* `current` inputs are latched (`latch_I`) and act during the next step (NEST `y0` /
  `I_stim`). `I_e` acts immediately.
* Spike output at step `n` carries the NEST stamp `(n + 1) dt`; a projection with delay
  `d` steps delivers it at step `n + d` (arrival `(n + 1 + d) dt` = NEST).
* Refractoriness: `R = ceil(t_ref / dt)` (NEST `Time::get_steps()` rounds **up**). T2's
  `Refractory.counts` used `round`, which differs from NEST for `t_ref` not a multiple of
  `dt` (e.g. 0.25 ms at 0.1 ms: NEST 3 steps, `round` 2); T3 fixes it via
  `units.grid_steps`.

## 3. Devices (NEST `StimulationDevice::is_active`)

With `t_min = steps(origin + start)`, `t_max = steps(origin + stop)`:

* `poisson_generator`: emits `Poisson(rate * dt)` counts at step `n` iff
  `t_min < n <= t_max`; key = `ctx.key` (required). One count per unit and step, shared
  by all targets of the unit (NEST draws per target: use one unit per target).
* `spike_generator`: spike time `T` has stamp `S = steps(origin + T)` and is emitted at
  step `S - 1` iff `t_min < S <= t_max`. Duplicate times and `spike_multiplicities`
  **add up** (count > 1), fixing the goal-01 bug where duplicates were dropped.
* `dc_generator`: outputs `amplitude` at step `n` iff `t_min < n + 2 <= t_max` (the NEST
  current-generator shift); with the NEST minimal delay of one step and the receiver's
  latch this reproduces NEST's onset exactly.

## 4. G5 — error vs substeps and default substeps

Defaults (dt = 0.1 ms): **`iaf_cond_exp`: `RK(4, 2)`**, **`aeif_cond_alpha`: `RK(4, 16)`**
(override per instance with `integrator=RK(4, k)`). Data and the open question are in §6.

## 5. Tests

* Single-neuron parity vs live NEST 3.10 (`requires_nest`): DC (`I_e`) traces and spike
  times, spike-input PSP traces (NEST `spike_generator` with delay = dt, our input given
  at the arrival step `round((T + d) / dt) - 1`, no extra alignment), `dc_generator`
  onset through the `current` latch, refractory with `t_ref` off-grid.
* `LIF` vs `_brainpy.LIF`: identical spikes; voltage equal to the old LIF's pre-reset
  value shifted by the reset; old LIF receives the current one step later (latch).
* Gradients: float64 BPTT vs central finite differences for `LIF` and `iaf_psc_alpha`;
  braintrace `compile` on an `LIF` net gives non-zero weight gradients.
* Devices: Poisson rate statistics, multiplicity counts, onset timing, jit/vmap/for_loop.
* float32 vs float64, refractory edge cases, heterogeneous parameters.

Extra points for `aeif_cond_alpha/dc` (same script, more substeps): k = 32: 1.572 mV full /
1.149e-02 excl.; k = 64: 0.868 / 5.081e-03 (spike times equal). Compile + run time of the
200 ms single-neuron rollout grows quickly with k (remote CPU: 0.4 s at k = 4, 7.8 s at
16, 52 s at 32, 182 s at 64), because substeps are Python-unrolled.

Findings:

* **iaf_cond_exp** meets CAT_A with 1 substep (strict 1e-3 mV absolute too); order-4
  convergence until NEST's own RKF45 error floor (~9e-6 mV with `gsl_error_tol = 1e-3`).
  Spike checks happen once per step on both sides, so spikes are identical. Default 2
  (50x margin under 1e-3 mV).
* **aeif_cond_alpha, subthreshold**: order-4 convergence (ratio ~20 from k = 1 to 2),
  1e-3 mV met from k = 2.
* **aeif_cond_alpha, spiking**: the full-trace error is dominated by the explosive
  upswing and the reset: the threshold crossing is located only to within one substep,
  so the spike/reset time error is O(h) (**first order**), and on the upswing
  (dV/dt ~ 10^2 to 10^10 mV/ms) a time shift of a few microseconds is ~1 mV. Off the spike
  steps the error converges at first order (0.26 -> 0.018 mV for k = 1 -> 16) and meets
  the CAT_A bound from k = 8; spike times equal NEST from k = 16. **G5 as written ("error
  decreases at the integrator's order; default substeps meet CAT_A") cannot be met for
  spiking aeif traces with fixed-substep RK4** -- escalated (PR body). Options: (a) G5 for
  spiking aeif = spike times equal + CAT_A off the spike steps (met at k = 16, the chosen
  default); (b) in-substep event location (interpolate the crossing time inside the
  substep, integrate the remainder from `V_reset`) in T2's integrator/SpikeRule, which
  restores higher order; (c) much larger k (still ~0.9 mV at k = 64, and compile time
  explodes).
